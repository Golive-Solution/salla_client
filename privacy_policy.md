# Privacy Policy

Effective Date: [30-09-2025]

Thank you for using the **Salla ↔ ERPNext Integration App** (“the App”).  
Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle data when you use the App.

---

## 1. Information We Collect
The App may collect and process the following types of information:
- **ERPNext Data**: Item master, price list, stock balance, orders, and customer details required for synchronization.  
- **Salla Data**: Customer master data, order details, and updates required for synchronization.  
- **Integration Credentials**: API keys or access tokens necessary to connect ERPNext with Salla.

---

## 2. How We Use Information
We use the collected data solely for:
- Synchronizing items, stock, customers, and orders between ERPNext and Salla.  
- Ensuring real-time updates between both platforms.  
- Generating documents in ERPNext (POS Invoice, Sales Invoice, Sales Order) based on Salla orders.  

---

## 3. Data Sharing
- We **do not sell, rent, or share** your data with third parties.  
- Data is transmitted only between **ERPNext** and **Salla** through secure API communication.
- Client App hosted in your **ERPNext** and **Server** app Hosted in a frappe site to synch data between two ends 

---

## 4. Data Security
- All communication between ERPNext and Salla is performed using encrypted HTTPS protocols.  
- API keys and credentials are stored securely in your ERPNext instance and in Server App as well.  
- We recommend restricting access to authorized users only.  

---

## 5. Data Retention
- The App does not store your business data outside your ERPNext instance.  
- Data is retained only as long as necessary for synchronization purposes.  

---

## 6. Your Responsibilities
- Keep your ERPNext and Salla credentials secure.  
- Review user access permissions regularly in ERPNext.  

---

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within the App repository.
